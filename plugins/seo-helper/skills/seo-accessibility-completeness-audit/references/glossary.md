# Glossary: Term Provenance

The seed article mixes three distinct vocabularies without labeling which is which: **(1)** terms Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR/Holistic SEO coined for their own SEO framework, **(2)** real Google terminology (from patents, official docs, or leaked internal docs), and **(3)** real academic/computer-science terminology borrowed from elsewhere. Conflating these three is the single biggest source of the article's credibility problems — it repeatedly presents (1) and (3) with the same authority as (2). This glossary sorts every major term in the article into its actual bucket.

**Practical takeaway:** when this article (or Koray's other writing) cites something as if it were established Google or academic fact, check which bucket it's actually in before treating it as verified. Buckets 2 (real Google terminology) and 3 (real academic terminology) are independently checkable against primary sources — bucket 1 (Koray/Holistic-SEO framework) is a practitioner's own model, useful and internally consistent on its own terms, but not independently validated the way the article's rhetoric sometimes implies.
